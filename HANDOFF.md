## Remaining #336 acceptance work

Issue #336 stays open. The full original-ID code-owned semantic/lesson catalog, generic self-maintaining constructors, canonical law admission and the previously identified 33 typed-but-not-source-proven cross-world composition positions remain independent architecture work. Peer/champion-relative and statistically calibrated S2 baselines also remain open.

The remaining teacher-policy decision is material and must not be guessed. Historical read-only reconstruction established that legacy `old` means a literal count of **separate earlier-grade-origin questions** in a 32-example batch, `preview` means separate future-grade questions, and `distill` is a KL coefficient against a frozen pre-attempt parent on retention rows. Champion-voted current questions, failed-exam replay and embedded prerequisite constituents are different semantics. Mapping any of those onto `old`, `preview` or `distill` would change the learned policy's meaning and reward distribution. A diagnostic-conditioned option selector therefore needs its own versioned, source-attested policy/reward admission and explicit user confirmation before it may affect live human training.

**Teacher-choice review:** The inherited UCB chooses each policy key, but the incoming implementation passes a full batch-sized candidate capacity to `champion_directed_lessons`. A source-valid, balanced champion vote can therefore replace every initially sampled example within that policy. Synthetic review demonstrated this behavior; its effect on the trained student and appropriateness for learned teacher control remain unmeasured. Resolve that allocation in a separately authorized teacher-policy change, without inferring approval from this merge.

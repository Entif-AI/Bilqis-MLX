## Cardinal rules for **adding or editing** semantics, lessons, grades and tests

1. **Authority before alteration:** Read `README.md`, this `AGENTS.md`, append-only `ADR.md`, current `HANDOFF.md`, `IPR-0220` and issue #336; check source/branch, writer ownership, live saved student and protected original artifacts. Separate proposal, verified source, admitted release, executed training and actual mastery. Do not silently commit an unreviewed local experimental repair as policy.
2. **Primitives: source and identity:** Search the current official New Ithkuil **grammar and lexicon** before adding or changing a primitive; verify meaning, scope, legal form, arity, operand/result types and independent oracle. Where morphology is unproven, explicitly use a scoped typed formal adapter. The Token Atlas's original token IDs are one-to-one and immutable; approved meaning changes receive a **new version plus compatible migration**, not an in-place reinterpretation or model reset. Allocate brand-new IDs only through separately admitted append-only vocabulary growth.
3. **Primitives: propagate edits:** On creation, semantic refinement or deprecation, update the canonical versioned registry/typed AST and prerequisite/dependent graph, track source/contract hashes, regenerate **affected** lesson/probe/test families and their source witnesses, and retain old release/generator receipts for already trained checkpoints. Nonsemantic editorial edits must not silently change active rubric identity; unknown or unverified semantics remain unadmitted.
4. **Lessons: dependency admission:** Before authoring a lesson, declare its stable capability ID, grade, prerequisites with **earlier demonstrated constituent mastery**, typed output/answer modes, smallest meaningful introduction, AST composition/complexity progression, legal/illegal boundaries and source authority. Never examine untaught roots or test newly composed concepts before separately teaching/assessing their required constituents.
5. **Lessons: compile, do not hand-patch:** Compile varied constructions from the approved semantic AST/DAG and source oracle, not a private Grade-B-only generator branch or fixed Q/A bank; create direct constituents, progressive compositions, decomposition/reconstruction, contrasts, inverses/metamorphic and negative cases **where semantically lawful**. Draw fresh seeded specimens per instructional visit **and optimizer step**; previous exam errors remain eligible to teach, never permanently embargoed.
6. **Lessons: coverage obligations:** Compute relevant predecessor and cross-grade semantic edges and source-valid construction families; cover *all required* types, truth cases, source-defined exceptional/boundary forms and appropriate answer modes (not just binary where a source supports numeric/native tokens). Report exact missing or unsupported edges as **coverage debt** and block only the unjustified new admission, not unrelated authorized progress. Random variety or a large item count does not prove coverage.
7. **Teaching feedback: no silent starvation:** Persist unresolved source-verified exam mistakes across changing sampled cohorts, with deduplication, correction evidence, fair exposure and visible bounded-memory eviction; do not overwrite failures merely because another exam occurred. Measure which concepts/examples actually received visits and durable subsequent improvement. Any queue size or scheduling ratio requires documented evidence; 24 per capability is only a **provisional local patch**, not universal doctrine.
8. **Teacher owns its learned policy:** Preserve teacher state, observations and policy selections. Supply verified instructional choices and evidence for prerequisite reinforcement; **do not force a fixed old/new lesson mix or override its selected strategy** on an assistant hypothesis. The recent up-to-25% Grade B prerequisite sample is an **unratified local experiment**, not a reusable requirement. **ADR-035 supersedes earlier direct prior-grade champion lesson votes:** only the frozen grade soup advises other grades, never individual capability or graduated-grade champion checkpoints; soup advice never alters oracle gold, independent exams, or the trainable student identity. Independently, current-grade capability-perfect champions may advise supervised answer confidence with small, gold-agreeing, breadth-proportional strength under the teacher-selected `distill` policy; the student must still pass all examinations unassisted (ADR-033). A capability's perfect champion is replaced only by a checkpoint with strictly more simultaneously perfect capabilities on the same complete student. Under ADR-034, a new coverage peak or displaced perfect champion immediately freezes a weights-only, capability-vote-weighted grade soup; its base votes equal its mastered grade-capability count, foreign-grade effective votes divide by the current number of school grades, and the causal student, matching AdamW and independent test scores remain unchanged by soups.
9. **Grades: spiral unchanged:** Admit roughly 6–9 similarly leveled prerequisite-compatible capabilities; run a full active-grade pass followed by **two fresh weakest-three rounds over all current and graduated capability grades**, allowing consecutive selection of a still-weak skill. Temporary earlier-grade regression during B/C/... is allowed; first reach 100% on current grade at one entire FP32 student+own AdamW, then replay A→...→current (Billy Madison), requiring cumulative same-checkpoint 100% before the next grade. Preserve grade champs, grade-vote multiplicity and original checkpoints.
10. **Tests: common rules, independent scoring:** Generate instruction and examination from the **same independently source-oracled semantic rules** with versioned seeds, AST/source/generator digests and corpus hashes. Sample varied positive/negative/invalid/contrastive witnesses consistent with source laws and support the capability's legitimate answer types; avoid artificial label balance that conceals semantics. No permanent sealed teaching exclusions. Never expose gold to inference; score every admitted capability on one intact checkpoint with its own AdamW, and compare challengers to incumbents only on **the identical sampled cohort** for promotion.
11. **Diagnostics: activate the user's five warnings:** Continuously observe (i) repetitive model-answer collapse separate from gold distribution; (ii) chance/lag with actual class baselines; (iii) transient reward vs durable learning; (iv) disconnected semantics/prerequisite defects; (v) unnecessarily complicated introductions. Require negative-control fixtures, per-capability/grade evidence, causal alternatives, teacher-readable diagnosis, observable follow-up and explicit `NOT MEASURED` when unknown. Merely rendering a dashboard or passing synthetic tests is **not** detection, intervention or neural mastery.
12. **Generated release and mutation-proof:** Property-test addition **and semantic editing** of a synthetic primitive, introduction of an unfamiliar dependent grade, inverse/counterexample coverage, typed result changes, intentionally missing sources/oracles, changing cohorts, mistake-queue starvation, and allowed temporary forgetting. Check affected-family regeneration, valid old receipt readback, no secret holdout, adaptive-teacher noninterference and source fail-closed behavior before any human-run recommendation.
13. **Custody and operator boundary:** Preserve the complete FP32 student, *its matching native AdamW*, teacher history, 222 historical token IDs, immutable grades/source manifests, student/grade champions, 100 GiB disk reserve and exact recovered state. Agents may run source/dry-run/disposable-model checks, **never** invoke actual `--human-run`, overwrite original checkpoints or change the human trainer's policy merely to quiet an alarm. Only the human starts or resumes real neural updates.
14. **Proof and handoff:** For every primitive/lesson/grade/test edit record source→typed AST/prerequisite→generated teaching/tests→coverage matrix→independent gold proof→five-signal telemetry→actual versioned release and disposable tests. Report what was observed versus unverified, track blockers without inventing authority, file BIOS incidents immediately, keep issue #336 and exact Git/issue/PM refs in **every handoff**, and do not claim future-proofing or real learning from green mock tests.

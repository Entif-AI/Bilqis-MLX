## Cardinal rules for **adding or editing** semantics, lessons, grades and tests

1. **Authority before alteration:** Read `README.md`, this `AGENTS.md`, append-only `ADR.md`, current `HANDOFF.md`, `IPR-0220` and issue #336; check source/branch, writer ownership, live saved student and protected original artifacts. Separate proposal, verified source, admitted release, executed training and actual mastery. Do not silently commit an unreviewed local experimental repair as policy.
2. **Primitives: source and identity:** Search the current official New Ithkuil **grammar and lexicon** before adding or changing a primitive; verify meaning, scope, legal form, arity, operand/result types and independent oracle. Where morphology is unproven, explicitly use a scoped typed formal adapter. The Token Atlas's original token IDs are one-to-one and immutable; approved meaning changes receive a **new version plus compatible migration**, not an in-place reinterpretation or model reset. Allocate brand-new IDs only through separately admitted append-only vocabulary growth.
3. **Primitives: propagate edits:** On creation, semantic refinement or deprecation, update the canonical versioned registry/typed AST and prerequisite/dependent graph, track source/contract hashes, regenerate **affected** lesson/probe/test families and their source witnesses, and retain old release/generator receipts for already trained checkpoints. Nonsemantic editorial edits must not silently change active rubric identity; unknown or unverified semantics remain unadmitted.
4. **Lessons: dependency admission:** Before authoring a lesson, declare its stable capability ID, grade, prerequisites with **earlier demonstrated constituent mastery**, typed output/answer modes, smallest meaningful introduction, AST composition/complexity progression, legal/illegal boundaries and source authority. Never examine untaught roots or test newly composed concepts before separately teaching/assessing their required constituents.
5. **Lessons: compile, do not hand-patch:** Compile varied constructions from the approved semantic AST/DAG and source oracle, not a private Grade-B-only generator branch or fixed Q/A bank; create direct constituents, progressive compositions, decomposition/reconstruction, contrasts, inverses/metamorphic and negative cases **where semantically lawful**. Draw fresh seeded specimens per instructional visit **and optimizer step**; previous exam errors remain eligible to teach, never permanently embargoed.
6. **Lessons: coverage obligations:** Compute relevant predecessor and cross-grade semantic edges and source-valid construction families; cover *all required* types, truth cases, source-defined exceptional/boundary forms and appropriate answer modes (not just binary where a source supports numeric/native tokens). Report exact missing or unsupported edges as **coverage debt** and block only the unjustified new admission, not unrelated authorized progress. Random variety or a large item count does not prove coverage.
7. **Teaching feedback: no silent starvation:** Persist unresolved source-verified exam mistakes across changing sampled cohorts, with deduplication, correction evidence, fair exposure and visible bounded-memory eviction; do not overwrite failures merely because another exam occurred. Measure which concepts/examples actually received visits and durable subsequent improvement. Any queue size or scheduling ratio requires documented evidence; 24 per capability is only a **provisional local patch**, not universal doctrine.
8. **Teacher owns its learned policy:** Preserve teacher state, observations and policy selections. Supply verified instructional choices and evidence for prerequisite reinforcement; **do not force a fixed old/new lesson mix or override its selected strategy** on an assistant hypothesis. The recent up-to-25% Grade B prerequisite sample is an **unratified local experiment**, not a reusable requirement. Under SPEC-0002 revision 5, direct maxed-capability champion advice is same-grade only; frozen grade soups remain the championship-derived cross-grade aggregate voice with the existing foreign-grade dilution. All direct and soup advice remains teacher-`distill`-scaled and bounded; it never changes oracle gold, independent exams, scheduler selection, the causal student identity, or AdamW.
9. **Grades: sweep imperfect grades before global recovery:** Admit roughly 6–9 similarly leveled prerequisite-compatible capabilities. Run the complete currently introduced `MAIN` sequence for **every currently imperfect eligible admitted grade** in canonical wrapped order, skipping grades already at 100% on fresh cumulative evidence. Only after that ordinary imperfect-grade sweep is exhausted may the scheduler run at most **two fresh weakest-three rounds over the complete eligible cumulative pool**, allowing consecutive selection of a still-weak skill. After round two, reset the sweep and resume ordinary grade progression. Never run two recovery bursts after each individual grade, and never turn the bounded weakest-three mechanism into an indefinite current-letter loop. Temporary earlier-grade regression during B/C/... is allowed; first reach 100% on the current graduation grade at one entire FP32 student+own AdamW, then preserve ordered A→...→current Billy Madison qualification and cumulative same-checkpoint 100% before the next grade. Preserve grade champs, grade-vote multiplicity and original checkpoints. See ADR-061, which narrows ADR-059's cadence.
10. **Tests: common rules, independent scoring:** Generate instruction and examination from the **same independently source-oracled semantic rules** with versioned seeds, AST/source/generator digests and corpus hashes. Sample varied positive/negative/invalid/contrastive witnesses consistent with source laws and support the capability's legitimate answer types; avoid artificial label balance that conceals semantics. No permanent sealed teaching exclusions. Never expose gold to inference; score every admitted capability on one intact checkpoint with its own AdamW, and compare challengers to incumbents only on **the identical sampled cohort** for promotion.
11. **Diagnostics: activate the user's five warnings:** Continuously observe (i) repetitive model-answer collapse separate from gold distribution; (ii) chance/lag with actual class baselines; (iii) transient reward vs durable learning; (iv) disconnected semantics/prerequisite defects; (v) unnecessarily complicated introductions.

# Bilqis grade research records

This directory records each **admitted letter grade** as a research and engineering
history. Its reports connect the curriculum's semantic scope, the human-owned
training evidence, method changes, qualifications and unresolved experiments.
They are not interchangeable with the executable curriculum, historical
proposals, model checkpoint binaries or paper manuscripts.

## Rules for subsequent reports

Keep a separate dated report for each new grade and preserve earlier reports
when methods change. Identify the exact source and training release; distinguish
the observed checkpoint, a generated exam cohort and historical assessments.
Record paired same-cohort comparisons separately from scores obtained on
different samples. State the source of elapsed times and performance claims,
including disagreements between the console, event ledger and operator notes.

Every report should explain why curriculum/optimizer/teacher policy was changed
or deliberately left alone, what evidence justified its admission, what the
student actually learned according to the admitted assessment, and what was
**not** measured. Link only source-bound records. Never include model binaries,
private credentials or invented provenance in Git.

Later grades should reuse the proven Grade A teacher, cohort, champion,
checkpoint, and School50 machinery. An earlier grade's admission contract must
not be changed simply because the next grade needs new semantics.

# B05.4 | Seal completed-backbone depth summaries with producer provenance

**Program:** BAMF-0.2 root #341. **Lane:** B. **Parent:** B05 #355. **Originating blocker:** B05.1 #449.

## Outcome
Make each depth summary consumed by Block AttnRes provably originate from the declared completed backbone group of the exact model/generation/prediction boundary, rather than accepting a caller-labelled raw `DepthSummary` tensor.

B05.1's numerical/source-feature repair is green, but the negative witness shows a synthetic g2 tensor can be relabelled as g1 and accepted. The missing contract is provenance/custody, not selector mathematics.

## Authority and constraints
- Preserve the B05 depth equations and tensor shapes exactly.
- A summary identity must bind at least model topology/implementation identity, generation identity, prediction/input boundary, group index, source tensor identity/digest, and the ordering of available summaries.
- A consumer may not mint or relabel a trusted summary by supplying matching metadata beside an arbitrary FP32 tensor.
- The capture primitive must be usable by the eventual B11 full-model forward without making B05 depend on B11 for authority.
- h0/g1/g2/g3 remain distinct source positions; unavailable future groups remain absent.
- No new trainable tensors or alternate model definition are introduced.

## Required deliverable
Define the producer-side sealed summary/capture record and the only production constructor accepted by `depth_select`. Keep any raw-array path private/test-only.

## Acceptance
- Capture real h0/g1/g2/g3 fixtures at the declared boundaries and consume them successfully.
- Relabelling g2 bytes as g1 fails before selector computation.
- Same group index from a different generation/model/prediction boundary fails or produces a distinct sealed identity as appropriate.
- Future group summaries cannot be presented at an earlier boundary.
- Reordering available summaries changes/rejects identity rather than silently changing semantics.
- Existing B05.1 numerical/source-feature tests remain green.
- B11 can later emit these sealed records directly from the real 12-layer forward.
- Fresh independent EGM required before integration.

## Dependency / handoff
Consumes B01 topology/tensor identities and B04 execution/cache identity. B05.1 #449 must re-run its synthetic-summary negative witness against this contract and close only after the forged relabelling is rejected.

No live learner or optimizer start is authorized.

## Producer-authority correction | 29 October 2026

The original constraint that this capture be usable by B11 **without making B05 depend on B11 for producer authority is superseded by executable evidence**. B05 is the consumer/selector and cannot prove that arbitrary hidden bytes came from a completed backbone group. `AttentionScope`/`AttentionCache`, process-local tokens, object identity, caller labels and hashes of caller-provided hidden bytes do not establish that origin.

The missing producer authority is now owned by [B11.1a #586](https://github.com/Entif-AI/Bilqis-MLX/issues/586). Until #586 is implemented and independently reviewed, B05.4's production admission remains fail-closed. B05.4 continues to own selector-side validation and the g2-as-g1/cross-parameter-tree adversaries, but the positive producer receipt must originate from B11 execution rather than a B05 capture constructor.

Canonical interim implementation posture is draft PR #584 (`BLOCKED_PRODUCER_DEPENDENCY`). Draft PR #582 is exploratory/superseded for producer authority and must not be merged as an independent source of trust.

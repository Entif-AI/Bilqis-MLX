**A08.1a source-authority consumption clarification (2026-10-29):**
An A08 inventory, candidate or lesson set is a non-authoritative
proposal even when produced from a pinned source. Before installing
any program or accepting a source-fact check, the Factory registry
must take the separately governed A01 `AtlasArtifactSelection`
and rederive/compare the exact proposal with
`assert_family_inventory_current`,
`assert_curriculum_candidate_current` or
`assert_source_lesson_set_current` at the consuming boundary.
No caller-mintable schema, status or digest substitutes for this
source replay. Current A13 tenant/purpose/epoch/visibility/revocation
rights must then be checked independently. A source-valid proposal
without those rights remains non-executable and
`LEARNING_UNVERIFIED`.

**Source-owner prerequisites surfaced by independent review:**
The current A01 artifact has source-row keys/loci and a global
`source_rows_sha256`, but not raw source-row payload or per-edge
relation source-row provenance. A03's released adapters cover
formal A/B/C, not SKOS/Ithkuil source rows. Do not invent
`SourceSpan`, `FrameInstance`, `ContextEligibility` or
`SourceGrant` inside A08. Decompose the missing work into:
(1) source-owner resolution of exact pinned row bytes;
(2) a released SKOS/Ithkuil row-to-A03 frame adapter with truthful
occurrence/span/source reference;
(3) per-edge relation evidence binding where applicable;
(4) source-owner A13 grant issuance for frame/member evidence;
(5) a lesson-to-fresh-`ApprovedContextProjection` crosswalk
covering **both positive and contrast** source facts.

The final gate must call A13 `project_context` under the current
TaskScope, SourceFrontier and ContextPolicy, distinguishing
implementation-uncovered from source-held/revoked. A cheap
mandatory regression: authorize both positive and contrast
evidence, then revoke **only** the contrast grant. The same
lesson must become ineligible without losing the frozen A08
denominator or granting learner credit.

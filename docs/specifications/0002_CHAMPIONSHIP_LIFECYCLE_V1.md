# SPEC-0002: Bilqis championship, graduation reset, and frozen-mentor specification

**Status:** Normative user-directed contract, revision 6 (2026-10-19).
**Scope:** Bilqis MLX human-run school; source-oracled grade exams, same-grade champion roles, defended title batteries, soups and curriculum epochs.
**Authority:** User's 2026-10-17 through 2026-10-19 corrections, refined by the explicit empty-championship reset, staged grade-completion instructions, experiment-baseline challenge and defended-title battery succession. Revision 5 paused cross-grade individual-champion competition and direct cross-grade champion advice. Revision 6 makes that isolation strict for active title ownership and makes every settled checkpoint-pair title fight permanently idempotent. Frozen soups remain the cross-grade influence mechanism. ADR-032 through ADR-035, ADR-041, ADR-044, ADR-047, ADR-051 through ADR-058, ADR-062, ADR-068 and protected IPR-0220 remain provenance where not superseded here. This supersedes conflicting historical champion and active-mentor rules but never rewrites their evidence. Document approval is separate from runtime conformance.

## 1. Definitions and state boundaries

The **causal student** is one complete FP32 neural checkpoint with **its own matching native AdamW**. Only the human launches actual neural updates. Candidate admission, graduation, championship promotion and mentor eligibility are different decisions.

- **Capability best:** A source-scored per-capability high-water result. It may be below 100% and remains useful archival/diagnostic evidence even when it is not active teaching authority.
- **Capability champion:** The one active best-scoring owner of that capability within the current championship epoch, on a complete student with its own AdamW. A champion may be below 100%. One checkpoint may hold many roles.
- **Maxed capability champion:** An active capability champion with verified current or historical perfect-question evidence sufficient for its existing direct advisory entitlement. Title succession is nevertheless governed by the current superficial gate and defended-title battery below; historical perfection is not a numerical title floor.
- **Grade championship:** The active collection of one grade's individual capability roles. Capability titles remain independently contestable even when one checkpoint owns several of them.
- **Title grade:** The grade whose capability is being championed. An active role is valid only when the checkpoint earned that role from a checkpoint whose retained optimizer update originated in the same grade. Evaluation in another grade does not re-label a frozen checkpoint's origin.
- **Settled matchup:** One completed title fight for one capability between one unordered pair of checkpoint SHA identities. Once settled, that pair can never fight each other again for that capability in this run, regardless of later score drift, role reversal, title transfer, fresh cohorts, new batteries, or any other changed condition.
- **School score:** Correct answers across **every admitted capability in the cumulative school assessment scope** on one complete checkpoint. Record numerator and denominator, never a bare percentage.
- **Epoch baseline:** The one complete graduation checkpoint, or its exact geometry-compatible post-admission clone, retained as a provenance, custody, and progress reference when new curriculum semantics are admitted. **It holds zero active championship roles and casts zero teacher votes.** It must not become a fallback adviser when no new champion has yet been earned.
- **Frozen grade soup:** Separate immutable weights-only teacher formed from **active, earned** grade roles. It is neither the causal student nor the epoch baseline.
- **Provenance archive:** Old championship pointers, soups, rubrics, exams, roles, checkpoints and receipts. Supersession does not authorize deletion.

## 2. Graduation and the curriculum-geometry reset

The normal epoch-reset trigger is **admission of new semantics or capabilities after graduation**. Routine lessons, challenger acceptance, exams, soup refreshes, poor progress and drift MUST NOT trigger a reset. A numeric scope change is not automatically a new semantic-grade epoch.

Graduation requires **one intact causal student with its own AdamW scoring 100% on the cumulative admitted school** after the prescribed A-through-current revalidation. No combination of per-grade or per-capability champions may manufacture that result.

At the ensuing new-curriculum admission boundary:

1. Verify the graduating checkpoint, own AdamW, complete cumulative exam receipt, old and new semantic graphs and admitted grade.
2. Set **all active championship roles and all active grade soups to empty** at the new epoch boundary; save an explicit, versioned reset receipt and the single graduation-derived baseline **as a non-teaching reference only**. A post-admission clone binds exactly to that graduate and preserves existing parameter and optimizer state.
3. Remove all prior-epoch champions and frozen soups from **active teaching guidance**, regardless of historical breadth, source scores or votes. Preserve the original pointers, files and signed/scored evidence for history and recovery. Until a new complete checkpoint earns a role, **no champion and no soup offers advice**. Do not load the baseline as a fallback teacher.
4. Do not fabricate roles, mastery or voting entitlements for any previously mastered or newly introduced capability. A baseline must never masquerade as one or several earned champions. After the reset, later complete capability-local scoring may establish an empty capability role at the best measured score even when that score is below 100%.
5. Continue the **causal student** from its latest legitimate committed model/AdamW lineage. Resetting active championship *references* MUST NOT change student weights, overwrite its AdamW, substitute an archive, or make optimizer updates.

Previously mastered grades can acquire new championship owners in this epoch only through section 3. New-epoch soups are rebuilt from newly earned active roles. Source-graph drift, absent graduation evidence or broken custody MUST fail closed, never revert silently to historical mentors.

**This is a simultaneous graduation-boundary reset to ZERO active championships**, not a gradual weighting schedule in which obsolete champions retain teaching influence while newer ones emerge.

**Explicit one-time experimental exception:** The human may request an administrative empty-roster reset **mid-grade** on an already isolated trial run. It is a metadata-only atomic event, not a second graduation, new curriculum admission, optimizer rollback, or model copy. Archive the trial's then-active championship roles and soups, clear all active champion/soup/baseline guidance, retain the complete existing student and native AdamW, and require a **strictly later optimizer step** before fresh examinations may earn any new role. The reset operation is idempotent and must not race an active trainer process. An ordinary training resume never implicitly resets an already established new roster.

**Explicit experiment-baseline rebase:** On direct human instruction for an already isolated trial, a previously verified complete student may replace the trial's active student as an **independent experiment-local clone**, while the canonical source bundle remains read-only. Restore only source-compatible scheduler/exam state belonging to that exact checkpoint, preserve the experiment's evolved teacher unless separately directed, archive the displaced epoch authority, and reset active roles/soups to empty. A rebase may point backward in optimizer-step history only when it remains later than the prior graduated checkpoint and is explicitly receipted as a rebase rather than an ordinary continuation.

An experiment rebase keeps the replacement baseline non-teaching and outside the active role roster. It is a custody/progress reference, not a fictitious incumbent and not a global embargo on later champions. The baseline checkpoint itself cannot immediately reclaim roles merely because it has saved scores; a strictly later eligible checkpoint must supply complete comparable grade evidence. Empty roles may then be established by the best measured capability scores under section 3, including scores below 100%. Any historical `baseline_challenge_required` field is non-authoritative for this best-score rule and MUST NOT block an otherwise valid empty-role promotion.

## 3. Current-cohort qualification and defended title batteries

Normal non-flash examinations may establish an empty capability role from complete source-scored evidence for that capability, including a below-perfect first best, without waiting for grade graduation. An occupied capability can be challenged as soon as the intact contender checkpoint has a complete comparable panel for **that capability**. In particular, any same-grade contender that reaches the capability ceiling is admitted to that capability's title match even if it has not simultaneously maxed, tested or surpassed every other title owned by the incumbent checkpoint. Ownership of several roles by one checkpoint MUST NOT turn them into one indivisible admission gate.

The contender checkpoint's **origin grade must equal the title grade**. The incumbent role must satisfy the same invariant. A frozen checkpoint that was last created/retained by Grade B training cannot own or defend an A capability title merely because it later scores well on A questions. A checkpoint is not reclassified by being examined while another grade is active. Legacy active roles whose checkpoint origin grade does not match the capability grade are invalid active authority: archive them, remove them from the active role/soup roster, and leave the role empty until a valid same-grade checkpoint earns it. If origin grade cannot be proven from durable checkpoint/state/event provenance, fail closed rather than treating the role as same-grade.

Stagnation quarantine is **teaching/scheduling authority, not championship authority**. A quarantined or withheld capability may be omitted from a particular scored pool, but that MUST NOT suppress championship processing for other same-grade capabilities that were completely and source-validly measured at the intact checkpoint. Any such capability that reaches its title-admission condition, including `12/12`, remains eligible to mint an empty same-grade role or challenge a valid incumbent. Never turn `blocked_before`/`blocked_after` for one capability into a blanket championship embargo for the whole exam.

An incumbent retains its title and same-grade advisory entitlement until a challenger qualifies. A weaker retest does not itself demote it. The first stage is a **superficial current-cohort qualification gate** on one target capability: incumbent and contender are scored on identical current questions, never against an old historical score as a numerical floor. A strictly higher comparable capability score qualifies the contender. Reaching the capability ceiling also qualifies the contender even when the incumbent currently ties that ceiling; the defended battery, not the incumbent checkpoint's ownership of other capabilities, decides the title. The current-cohort result alone does **not** transfer the title.

The qualification gate is capability-local. Greater same-grade breadth or school-wide correctness may remain diagnostic context, but neither is required merely to earn a title match once the contender has hit that capability's ceiling. A below-ceiling superficial tie does not qualify the contender. Cross-grade champions do not enter the comparison.

Each active capability championship owns a **surviving title battery** of exact, source-oracled question identities. The provenance archive remains append-only, but the active battery is not a monotonically growing ancestral obligation. Once a contender clears the superficial gate, incumbent and contender enter a fresh head-to-head title match over that capability's current surviving battery. Both frozen checkpoints MUST perform fresh neural inference in the title match. Cached model predictions, including predictions previously produced by the same checkpoint for the same question, MUST NOT be used to adjudicate the match. Deterministic source/oracle answers and immutable question construction may be memoized and reused; neural inference results may not.

Before scheduling any title match, check the immutable settled-matchup ledger using `(capability, sorted(checkpoint_sha256_pair))`. If that key already exists, the contender is ineligible to fight that incumbent for that capability again. Do not re-run the superficial championship comparison, do not re-run the title battery, do not refresh either role's displayed score from that pairing, and do not mutate the surviving battery. This is permanent pairwise idempotence, not a cooldown. The same two checkpoints may still have one separate first-ever fight for a different capability because capability titles are independent.

The title match is comparative, not pass/fail perfection. Whichever checkpoint answers **more of the identical surviving battery correctly** owns the capability after the match; a battery-score tie preserves the incumbent. Once the contender qualified for the title match, its later fresh score may fall below the superficial qualification threshold and it may still win by outperforming the incumbent on the full surviving battery. Conversely, the incumbent must freshly defend the battery and cannot retain a title merely because of historical peak scores.

The title match also refreshes the winner's **current championship evidence**. Both checkpoints freshly replay the current qualifying cohort as part of the match transaction; this replay does not re-run the superficial gate and cannot disqualify an already-qualified contender. The winner's score from this latest fresh current-cohort replay becomes the role's displayed/current score and the score used when superficially comparing the next contender. A defending champion may therefore retain the title while its current role score falls, for example from historical `12/12` to current `11/12`.

After every completed external title match, build the next surviving battery from the **winner's latest winning questions**: the deduplicated union of (a) questions the winner answered correctly in the original superficial current-cohort gate, (b) questions it answered correctly in the fresh current-cohort replay performed during the title match, and (c) questions it answered correctly from the prior surviving battery. A question remains in immutable history even if the winner now misses it, but it ceases to be an active title obligation unless the winner answered it correctly in one of those latest winning passes. The active bar can therefore strengthen, weaken, or change composition while historical evidence remains lossless.

Surviving batteries are owned **per capability championship**. Merely sharing one checkpoint does not merge unrelated capability batteries. Grade-wide or whole-school questions belong to a capability battery only when that broader evidence actually participated in qualifying or deciding that capability's title. Direct parent and transitive ancestor checkpoint identities remain archival provenance, not an ever-growing active pass/fail battery.

### Same-grade one-hop fan-out after a takeover

When a contender **actually wins** one capability title, perform exactly one bounded same-grade fan-out screen for that winning checkpoint. Quickly pair that frozen checkpoint against the incumbents of the other capabilities in the same grade on comparable current questions. For every other capability where the new champion **matches or beats** that incumbent's superficial score, queue a normal defended-title battery match for that capability as well. This screen exists to discover that one checkpoint may legitimately deserve several same-grade belts without requiring it to have maxed the entire grade before its first challenge.

The fan-out is **one-hop and non-recursive**. It is triggered only by the original title takeover. Winning a second, third or later capability from that queued fan-out MUST NOT trigger another scan of the grade, another fan-out generation, or any recursive cascade. All secondary matches are siblings in the original takeover transaction and are individually adjudicated by their own capability batteries.

The fan-out MUST also respect the settled-matchup ledger. A sibling capability already settled between those same two checkpoint SHAs is skipped without inference and cannot be queued again.

Cross-grade individual-champion challenges and ownership are disabled. A checkpoint does not challenge or retain capability titles in another grade merely because it is strong school-wide, and a same-grade fan-out never leaves its grade. Legacy cross-grade role ownership is archived and inactivated on migration. Cross-grade title retirement or consolidation may be reconsidered later as a separate product decision.

A same-checkpoint examination may refresh current score or perfect-capability evidence without displacing itself, but it does not create a synthetic external title match. Simultaneous breadth remains the result of one comparable grade examination; it is never synthesized by unioning perfect scores from different cohorts.

This championship-only title-match requirement does not add a candidate-retention veto, change the teacher's policy, or relax graduation. Flash retains its existing deferred-title behavior.

## 4. Comparable evidence and decision order

Every promotion decision binds the intact student/AdamW checkpoint SHA and optimizer step; admitted graph and grade/capability set; versioned source and oracle; fixed cohort digest/seed; rubric and assessment scope digests; per-capability grade scores; incumbents' exact identities; and any incumbent-versus-contender same-cohort school outcomes. Cumulative numerator and denominator are mandatory **when an actual incumbent comparison occurs**; they are not invented for an uncontested, grade-only championship.

Different cohorts, school scopes, numeric test domains, source contracts, rubric versions or graph geometries are **different comparison strata**. Even matching denominators do not make two independent question sets directly comparable. A title challenge requires comparable evidence for the target capability; the one-hop fan-out requires comparable evidence for each additional same-grade capability it screens. An append-compatible historical graph may compete only where it can read the complete target panel and battery. If the required comparison cannot be made fairly, that displacement is unmeasured and MUST NOT be approved. Keep the older record as historical evidence.

The independent complete school exam owns graduation and may also supply capability-local championship evidence when its target panel is intact and comparable. Short paired parent/challenger sentinels own **provisional student update admission only**. Championship qualification cannot combine scores from different checkpoint identities or incomparable cohorts. Whole-student high-water scores are separately ratcheting recovery/diagnostic references, not automatic championship ownership. A successful local teaching update is not graduation.

Order: validate checkpoint origin grade, provenance and custody; archive/inactivate any active role whose checkpoint origin grade does not equal the title grade; establish empty roles from complete capability evidence only when the contender's origin grade equals that title grade; treat quarantine/withholding as capability-local scored-scope information rather than a global championship stop; for every completely measured eligible same-grade capability, check the settled-matchup ledger before any inference; if unseen, pair incumbent and contender on the target capability's current cohort and apply the superficial strict-gain-or-contender-ceiling qualification rule; only qualified first-time pairs enter a title match; freshly infer **both** incumbent and contender on the current cohort for latest championship evidence and on that capability's surviving battery for title adjudication; award the capability to the higher battery score, with ties preserving the incumbent; refresh the winner's current score and surviving battery; write the immutable settled-matchup receipt. If and only if the contender actually takes that original title, run one same-grade fan-out screen against remaining valid incumbents, skipping already-settled pairs, queue matches where the new champion matches or beats their superficial scores, and adjudicate those sibling matches without recursion. Then commit immutable generation/outbox evidence and rebuild any affected soup. Never use cached neural predictions in title adjudication, never let an old peak score stand in for a live defense, never rematch a settled pair, never recurse fan-out, and never let the challenger become its own pre-promotion reference.

## 5. Frozen soups and teaching rights

When an **earned** role changes owner or covered capability count reaches a new maximum, rebuild the affected grade's frozen weights-only soup from **active earned** championship owners. Each grade capability contributes **one vote**. A checkpoint with multiple roles receives that many ingredient shares; it is loaded once but counted by role multiplicity.

A soup's base vote count is frozen with its recipe. Same-grade advice uses the full count. Soups remain the **only championship-derived cross-grade influence mechanism** and may retain the existing foreign-grade divisor.

Each active **maxed capability champion** contributes direct advisory influence only inside its own grade. The vote belongs to the capability role, not the checkpoint file. If one checkpoint owns several maxed roles in that grade, load it once and count one vote per qualifying role. A non-max capability champion contributes zero direct votes, but it remains a valid soup ingredient and soup vote under the existing best-role contract. Individual champions cast **no direct cross-grade votes** while revision 6 is active.

Direct and soup advice are restricted to independently source-gold-agreeing training examples, admitted answer types and the teacher's selected distillation strength with the bounded influence policy. Wrong mentor predictions contribute no advice for that example. Advice may not alter gold, exam answers, graduation, capability scheduling, the causal student's parameters or AdamW identity, or the teacher's selected lesson strategy. Never average AdamW moments, blend incompatible embedding geometries, or impose an assistant-selected prior-grade lesson proportion.

After an epoch reset, **old soups remain archived but inactive**. The baseline casts **zero votes and no direct advice**. Later newly earned roles can form new-epoch soups. A tie or refused takeover neither replaces an old incumbent in the active epoch nor silently triggers soup reconstruction.

## 6. Custody and human trial boundaries

Use existing immutable states, generation-bound outbox, protected bundle references, and post-commit retirement controls. Preserve the causal student, matching AdamW, teacher state, semantic version, source receipts, historic champions and soups. A temporary APFS experiment fork is review-pending and may only be retained or discarded after custody/provenance reconciliation. No implicit promotion of a fork into the canonical run.

Agents MAY run source checks, read-only current-run preflights, isolated disposable model and MLX fixture checks, and verified fork preparation. They MUST NOT start actual student optimizer updates or call the human-only neural training command. Only the human starts or resumes those updates.

## 7. Mandatory conformance tests

1. Reject false graduation assembled from mixed checkpoints or 99%-complete school scores. Successful graduation plus new admission resets **all active championships and grade soups to empty**, while retaining a single graduation-linked baseline as a **non-teaching** reference, without inventing mastery or deleting history.
2. Routine exams, candidate rejection and poor results **do not** reset the championship epoch.
3. An empty capability role accepts the first complete capability measurement even below 100%. Against an occupied same-grade role, a strict comparable capability-score gain qualifies the contender; reaching the capability ceiling also qualifies it even when the incumbent ties that ceiling. A below-ceiling superficial tie does not qualify it. Other roles owned by the same checkpoint cannot block admission to this one capability's title match.
4. Cross-grade individual-champion competition and active ownership are disabled. A role is active only if its checkpoint origin grade equals its capability grade. Legacy mismatches are archived/inactivated rather than grandfathered. Same-grade title qualification and adjudication never require taking over every role owned by one checkpoint or proving a cross-grade/full-school takeover first.
5. Cohort, target-capability panel, rubric or incompatible graph mismatch rejects promotion. Append-compatible historical checkpoints may compete only on complete readable target panels and surviving batteries. New or stale cohort scores never get compared as raw numbers.
6. A legitimate battery-match takeover updates exact individual roles and rebuilds the affected soup with correct vote multiplicity. A contender that fails the superficial gate or loses/ties the battery match preserves role ownership; a completed defense may still refresh the incumbent's current role score and surviving battery.
7. Historic champions and soups become inactive at curriculum admission; **the baseline supplies zero adviser models and zero votes**. Newly earned maxed capability roles advise directly only within their own grade. Newly earned soups advise with full same-grade base votes and the foreign-grade divisor and remain the cross-grade championship-derived influence mechanism. Non-max roles remain soup-only advisers. A non-training reset also clears then-active experimental titles and soups and leaves old artifacts archived.
8. The complete student, matching AdamW, teacher history and optimizer step remain unchanged by role reset, promotion or soup creation. Interrupted outbox delivery replays the same event without duplicate promotion.
9. The existing isolated fork passes read-only resume and source-bound inference before the human receives a test command. A locked atomic mid-grade empty-roster reset preserves native AdamW and all prior generations. No extra 8 GB copy is required. Fixture checks cannot establish real neural retention.
10. A capability title may be minted or challenged from complete capability-local evidence at one intact checkpoint; complete peer-capability scoring is not a prerequisite merely because the incumbent checkpoint owns several belts. Provenance, graph, rubric and source comparability still fail closed.
11. After one real same-grade title takeover, the winning checkpoint receives exactly one quick paired screen against the other incumbents in that grade. Every capability where it matches or beats the incumbent superficial score queues one sibling defended-title match. A win in any sibling match MUST NOT trigger another screen or queue generation.
12. No same-grade title action schedules a cross-grade individual-champion comparison. Cross-grade influence continues through frozen soups; direct champion advice remains same-grade only.

13. A superficially qualified contender and incumbent both perform fresh inference on the identical current cohort and identical incumbent surviving title battery. Neural predictions are never read from a cache for this adjudication. The higher battery score owns the capability; a tie preserves the incumbent. The winner's latest current-cohort replay supplies the role's displayed/current score even when it is lower than that checkpoint's historical peak. The next surviving battery is the deduplicated union of questions the winner answered correctly in the superficial gate, fresh current-cohort replay and prior battery replay. Missed historical questions remain archived but are not active obligations. Missing, altered or unverifiable question evidence fails closed.
14. The first completed title fight writes an immutable settled-matchup key for `(capability, unordered checkpoint pair)`. Any later attempt to pair those two checkpoint SHAs for that capability is skipped before championship inference and cannot change role score, owner or battery, even if either checkpoint's later exam score changes or ownership reverses.
15. Quarantine of B.AND, B.IFF or any other capability does not suppress a complete, source-valid A.IDENTITY `12/12` title challenge (and vice versa). Championship processing runs over the completely measured eligible capability subset; withheld capabilities simply do not mint/challenge from absent scores.

## 8. Runtime implementation and test-readiness rule

The historical `capability_frontier.graduation_perfect` retains its original breadth-only record format. The **active post-reset championship** is governed by `epoch_championships`: ordinary roles are current defended-title roles with strict checkpoint-origin/title-grade identity, capability-local superficial admission, fresh defended batteries, permanent pairwise matchup idempotence and one-hop same-grade fan-out after a real takeover. Cross-grade individual-title comparison and ownership are inactive. The role receipt must distinguish immutable historical provenance from its mutable-by-succession surviving title battery and latest defended current-cohort score, and the epoch must retain immutable settled-matchup receipts. In `trainer._take_exam`, normal fresh runs also initialize an earned-only epoch before graduation. Only independently earned same-grade roles and their newly frozen soups enter active advice. Each active maxed role contributes direct capability influence only in its own grade. A frozen soup gives one vote per active capability role, including a best-below-max role, and remains eligible for the existing foreign-grade divisor. Shared direct-role checkpoints may share one loaded immutable weight object for efficiency, but every first-ever title-match inference is fresh and must not reuse cached predicted answers. Deterministic source/oracle results may remain memoized independently. All advice remains independently source-gold-gated. Old `graduation_perfect` and `grade_soups` stay archived and protected from retirement. A-to-B and B-to-C admission initialize a **non-teaching** graduation baseline with no titles. An explicit administrative reset archives any current-epoch roles and starts empty. The teacher and single causal student still own their original policy, parameters and AdamW.

For the current isolated issue #340 recovery, the intended next-run roster starts empty except for the explicitly authorized recovery roles proven by the preserved 230216-versus-230232 fixed exam: B.AND, B.IMPLIES and B.NOT belong to the 230216 checkpoint because it strictly outscored 230232 on exactly those capabilities. Other roles remain empty until later complete capability-local evidence establishes their bests. This explicit preload does not declare those three capabilities mastered and does not fabricate roles for tied capabilities.

Do **not** label a human neural learning outcome proven from synthetic acceptance tests. Human launch requires the exact committed source SHA, zero-blocker read-only preflight of this existing run, no concurrent human trainer, and an explicit `--human-run --resume --run-dir` against the isolated folder. Compare observed school scores on their actual cohort identities, the count of earned active roles, soup ingredients and outcomes. The human decides the trial's final keep/discard disposition. Do not prune unique experimental or canonical evidence beforehand.

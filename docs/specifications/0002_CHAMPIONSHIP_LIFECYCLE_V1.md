# SPEC-0002: Bilqis championship, graduation reset, and frozen-mentor specification

**Status:** Normative user-directed contract, revision 10 (2026-10-19).
**Scope:** Bilqis MLX human-run school; source-oracled grade exams, capability-local champion roles, defended title batteries, soups and curriculum epochs.
**Authority:** User's 2026-10-17 through 2026-10-19 corrections, refined by the explicit empty-championship reset, staged grade-completion instructions, experiment-baseline challenge and defended-title battery succession. Revision 5 paused broad cross-grade champion competition and direct cross-grade champion advice. Revision 6 made title fights permanently idempotent and temporarily coupled active title ownership to the optimizer visit grade that produced a checkpoint. Revision 7 binds every active championship epoch to one exact holistic school identity; any incompatible school/curriculum/semantic/model-geometry change archives and clears **all** active champions and soups before further advice or title defense. Revision 8 requires at least 66% capability proficiency before an empty title can be minted and defines that first owner's battery as exactly the questions it answered correctly on the threshold-crossing capability exam. **Revision 9 separates checkpoint-training provenance from capability-title jurisdiction:** a whole-student checkpoint may mint or challenge any completely measured capability title in the unchanged school identity regardless of which grade's visit last retained that checkpoint. **Revision 10 makes title defense asymmetric in favor of the qualified challenger:** the incumbent retains the belt only by scoring strictly higher on the defended battery. A battery tie transfers the title to the challenger. The title remains owned by the capability's grade, fan-out remains within that title grade, and direct champion advice remains title-grade-local. Frozen soups remain the bounded cross-grade influence mechanism. ADR-032 through ADR-035, ADR-041, ADR-044, ADR-047, ADR-051 through ADR-058, ADR-062, ADR-068, ADR-071 and protected IPR-0220 remain provenance where not superseded here. This supersedes conflicting historical champion and active-mentor rules but never rewrites their evidence. Document approval is separate from runtime conformance.

## 1. Definitions and state boundaries

The **causal student** is one complete FP32 neural checkpoint with **its own matching native AdamW**. Only the human launches actual neural updates. Candidate admission, graduation, championship promotion and mentor eligibility are different decisions.

- **Capability best:** The current same-rubric comparable frontier pointer for one capability. Its event and immutable state receipt preserve the observed score, but the hot model bundle is protected only while some live custody surface still references it. A later fresh cohort can supersede this slot even with a lower raw score because raw scores from different cohorts are not a permanent total order.
- **Capability champion:** The active defended title owner for one capability within the current championship epoch, on a complete student with its own AdamW. Championship ownership is durable role authority, not a synonym for the numerically highest raw score ever observed. A champion may be below 100%, and one checkpoint may hold many independently defended roles.
- **Maxed capability champion:** An active capability champion with verified current or historical perfect-question evidence sufficient for its existing direct advisory entitlement. Title succession is nevertheless governed by the current superficial gate and defended-title battery below; historical perfection is not a numerical title floor.
- **Grade championship:** The active collection of one grade's individual capability roles. Capability titles remain independently contestable even when one checkpoint owns several of them.
- **Title grade:** The grade whose capability is being championed. Title jurisdiction follows the capability, not the optimizer visit that last changed the whole student. The role records the title grade and separately records the checkpoint's retained-update origin grade as provenance.
- **Settled matchup:** One completed title fight for one capability between one unordered pair of checkpoint SHA identities. Once settled, that pair can never fight each other again for that capability in this run, regardless of later score drift, role reversal, title transfer, fresh cohorts, new batteries, or any other changed condition.
- **Championship school identity:** The exact comparison geometry within which active roles and soups are valid. It is durably fingerprinted from the admitted `course.digest()`, the active factory release identity, the active semantic-graph digest, and the model parameter-shape inventory. These inputs bind school-wide capability/question structure, semantic vocabulary/meaning, executable assessment release and neural geometry.
- **School score:** Correct answers across **every admitted capability in the cumulative school assessment scope** on one complete checkpoint. Record numerator and denominator, never a bare percentage.
- **Epoch baseline:** The one complete graduation checkpoint, or its exact geometry-compatible post-admission clone, retained as a provenance, custody, and progress reference when new curriculum semantics are admitted. **It holds zero active championship roles and casts zero teacher votes.** It must not become a fallback adviser when no new champion has yet been earned.
- **Frozen grade soup:** Separate immutable weights-only teacher formed from **active, earned** grade roles. It is neither the causal student nor the epoch baseline.
- **Provenance archive:** Old championship pointers, soups, rubrics, exams, roles, checkpoints and receipts. Supersession does not authorize deletion.

## 2. Graduation and the curriculum-geometry reset

The normal epoch-reset trigger is **admission of new semantics or capabilities after graduation**. Routine lessons, challenger acceptance, exams, soup refreshes, poor progress and drift MUST NOT trigger a reset. A numeric scope change is not automatically a new semantic-grade epoch.

Graduation requires **one intact causal student with its own AdamW scoring 100% on the cumulative admitted school** after the prescribed A-through-current revalidation. No combination of per-grade or per-capability champions may manufacture that result.

At the ensuing new-curriculum admission boundary:

1. Verify the graduating checkpoint, own AdamW, complete cumulative exam receipt, old and new semantic graphs and admitted grade.
2. Set **all active championship roles and all active grade soups to empty** at the new epoch boundary; save an explicit, versioned reset receipt and the single graduation-derived baseline **as a non-teaching reference only**. A post-admission clone binds exactly to that graduate and preserves existing parameter and optimizer state.
3. Remove all prior-epoch champions and frozen soups from **active teaching guidance**, regardless of historical breadth, source scores or votes. Preserve the original pointers, files and signed/scored evidence for history and recovery. Until a new complete checkpoint earns a role, **no champion and no soup offers advice**. Do not load the baseline as a fallback teacher.
4. Do not fabricate roles, mastery or voting entitlements for any previously mastered or newly introduced capability. A baseline must never masquerade as one or several earned champions. After the reset, later complete capability-local scoring may establish an empty capability role only after the intact checkpoint reaches at least **66% proficiency** on that capability.
5. Continue the **causal student** from its latest legitimate committed model/AdamW lineage. Resetting active championship *references* MUST NOT change student weights, overwrite its AdamW, substitute an archive, or make optimizer updates.

Previously mastered grades can acquire new championship owners in this epoch only through section 3. New-epoch soups are rebuilt from newly earned active roles. Source-graph drift, absent graduation evidence or broken custody MUST fail closed, never revert silently to historical mentors.

**This is a simultaneous graduation-boundary reset to ZERO active championships**, not a gradual weighting schedule in which obsolete champions retain teaching influence while newer ones emerge.

**Explicit one-time experimental exception:** The human may request an administrative empty-roster reset **mid-grade** on an already isolated trial run. It is a metadata-only atomic event, not a second graduation, new curriculum admission, optimizer rollback, or model copy. Archive the trial's then-active championship roles and soups, clear all active champion/soup/baseline guidance, retain the complete existing student and native AdamW, and require a **strictly later optimizer step** before fresh examinations may earn any new role. The reset operation is idempotent and must not race an active trainer process. An ordinary training resume never implicitly resets an already established new roster.

**Explicit experiment-baseline rebase:** On direct human instruction for an already isolated trial, a previously verified complete student may replace the trial's active student as an **independent experiment-local clone**, while the canonical source bundle remains read-only. Restore only source-compatible scheduler/exam state belonging to that exact checkpoint, preserve the experiment's evolved teacher unless separately directed, archive the displaced epoch authority, and reset active roles/soups to empty. A rebase may point backward in optimizer-step history only when it remains later than the prior graduated checkpoint and is explicitly receipted as a rebase rather than an ordinary continuation.

An experiment rebase keeps the replacement baseline non-teaching and outside the active role roster. It is a custody/progress reference, not a fictitious incumbent and not a global embargo on later champions. The baseline checkpoint itself cannot immediately reclaim roles merely because it has saved scores; a strictly later eligible checkpoint must supply complete comparable capability evidence. Empty roles may then be established under section 3 only when the first eligible measurement reaches the 66% proficiency floor. Any historical `baseline_challenge_required` field is non-authoritative for this rule and MUST NOT block an otherwise valid empty-role promotion.

## 3. Current-cohort qualification and defended title batteries

Normal non-flash examinations may establish an empty capability role from complete source-scored evidence for that capability only when the checkpoint reaches at least **66% proficiency** on that capability. On the current twelve-question panels, 8/12 qualifies and 7/12 does not. This threshold applies only to minting an empty/cleared role. An occupied capability can be challenged as soon as the intact contender checkpoint has a complete comparable panel for **that capability**. In particular, any contender that reaches the capability ceiling is admitted to that capability's title match even if it has not simultaneously maxed, tested or surpassed every other title owned by the incumbent checkpoint. Ownership of several roles by one checkpoint MUST NOT turn them into one indivisible admission gate.

When an empty role is minted at the threshold, its initial surviving title battery is **exactly** the source-oracled questions for that capability that the new owner answered correctly on that threshold-crossing examination. Do not add its missed questions, sibling-capability questions, a whole-grade panel, older exams, synthetic padding or unrelated history. This exact passed-question set is the battery used when the first valid contender later challenges the title.

The checkpoint's retained-update **origin grade is provenance, not championship eligibility**. A checkpoint last changed during Grade A may mint or challenge a B capability title when that exact intact checkpoint supplies complete comparable B evidence, and vice versa. The active role's title grade remains the capability grade; the role separately records `checkpoint_origin_grade` so the optimizer history is never rewritten or disguised. Missing or invalid checkpoint-origin provenance still fails closed. This rule does not create cross-grade direct mentor votes: championship authority follows the capability title, not the checkpoint's training origin.

Stagnation quarantine is **teaching/scheduling authority, not championship authority**. A quarantined or withheld capability may be omitted from a particular scored pool, but that MUST NOT suppress championship processing for any other capability that was completely and source-validly measured at the intact checkpoint. Any such capability that reaches its title-admission condition, including `12/12`, remains eligible to mint an empty role or challenge a valid incumbent. Never turn `blocked_before`/`blocked_after` for one capability into a blanket championship embargo for the whole exam.

### Holistic school changes reset the entire championship field

Active champions and soups are meaningful only while the **championship school identity is unchanged**. Before any active role or soup may advise, qualify, defend or participate in fan-out, compare the epoch's stored school identity with the live one. Any mismatch invalidates the entire active field at once. Archive the current active roles, active soups and their receipts; clear all active roles and all active soups; bind the empty successor field to the new school identity; keep immutable historical evidence only as provenance, never as live teaching/title authority.

This reset is intentionally global, not capability-local. It applies whenever the school ceases to be apples-to-apples, including at minimum:

- any change to the admitted course digest, including the school-wide grade/capability inventory or any `exam_item_counts` that change the total question inventory;
- any change to the semantic graph digest, including the number or identity of active semantics/tokens;
- any change to model parameter shapes / neural geometry;
- any change to the active factory release identity that changes the executable school/assessment release.

Do not attempt to salvage, re-score or selectively grandfather prior active champions/soups across such a boundary. Their advice may be harmful precisely because the comparison geometry changed. A newly admitted grade or other holistic expansion starts with an empty active championship/soup field, even though the causal student and its own optimizer state continue. This global reset does **not** erase immutable championship history or the permanent settled-pair ledger; historical receipts remain auditable, but they provide no live authority in the new geometry.

An incumbent retains its title and title-grade advisory entitlement until a challenger qualifies. A weaker retest does not itself demote it. The first stage is a **superficial current-cohort qualification gate** on one target capability: incumbent and contender are scored on identical current questions, never against an old historical score as a numerical floor. A strictly higher comparable capability score qualifies the contender. Reaching the capability ceiling also qualifies the contender even when the incumbent currently ties that ceiling; the defended battery, not the incumbent checkpoint's ownership of other capabilities, decides the title. The current-cohort result alone does **not** transfer the title.

The qualification gate is capability-local. Greater title-grade breadth or school-wide correctness may remain diagnostic context, but neither is required merely to earn a title match once the contender has hit that capability's ceiling. A below-ceiling superficial tie does not qualify the contender. Only the incumbent of the same capability enters the primary comparison; the checkpoint's optimizer-origin grade is irrelevant to eligibility.

Each active capability championship owns a **surviving title battery** of exact, source-oracled question identities. The provenance archive remains append-only, but the active battery is not a monotonically growing ancestral obligation. Once a contender clears the superficial gate, incumbent and contender enter a fresh head-to-head title match over that capability's current surviving battery. Both frozen checkpoints MUST perform fresh neural inference in the title match. Cached model predictions, including predictions previously produced by the same checkpoint for the same question, MUST NOT be used to adjudicate the match. Deterministic source/oracle answers and immutable question construction may be memoized and reused; neural inference results may not.

Before scheduling any title match, check the immutable settled-matchup ledger using `(capability, sorted(checkpoint_sha256_pair))`. If that key already exists, the contender is ineligible to fight that incumbent for that capability again. Do not re-run the superficial championship comparison, do not re-run the title battery, do not refresh either role's displayed score from that pairing, and do not mutate the surviving battery. This is permanent pairwise idempotence, not a cooldown. The same two checkpoints may still have one separate first-ever fight for a different capability because capability titles are independent.

The title match is comparative, not pass/fail perfection. The incumbent retains the capability only when it answers **strictly more** of the identical surviving battery correctly than the challenger. If the challenger scores more, or the battery scores tie, the challenger owns the capability after the match. Once the contender qualified for the title match, its later fresh score may fall below the superficial qualification threshold and it may still win on the full surviving battery. The incumbent must freshly defend and cannot retain a title merely because of historical peak scores or a tied defense.

The title match also refreshes the winner's **current championship evidence**. Both checkpoints freshly replay the current qualifying cohort as part of the match transaction; this replay does not re-run the superficial gate and cannot disqualify an already-qualified contender. The winner's score from this latest fresh current-cohort replay becomes the role's displayed/current score and the score used when superficially comparing the next contender. A defending champion may therefore retain the title while its current role score falls, for example from historical `12/12` to current `11/12`.

After every completed external title match, build the next surviving battery from the **winner's latest winning questions**: the deduplicated union of (a) questions the winner answered correctly in the original superficial current-cohort gate, (b) questions it answered correctly in the fresh current-cohort replay performed during the title match, and (c) questions it answered correctly from the prior surviving battery. A question remains in immutable history even if the winner now misses it, but it ceases to be an active title obligation unless the winner answered it correctly in one of those latest winning passes. The active bar can therefore strengthen, weaken, or change composition while historical evidence remains lossless.

Surviving batteries are owned **per capability championship**. Merely sharing one checkpoint does not merge unrelated capability batteries. Grade-wide or whole-school questions belong to a capability battery only when that broader evidence actually participated in qualifying or deciding that capability's title. Direct parent and transitive ancestor checkpoint identities remain archival provenance, not an ever-growing active pass/fail battery.

### Same-grade one-hop fan-out after a takeover

When a contender **actually wins** one capability title, perform exactly one bounded same-grade fan-out screen for that winning checkpoint. Quickly pair that frozen checkpoint against the incumbents of the other capabilities in the same grade on comparable current questions. For every other capability where the new champion **matches or beats** that incumbent's superficial score, queue a normal defended-title battery match for that capability as well. This screen exists to discover that one checkpoint may legitimately deserve several same-grade belts without requiring it to have maxed the entire grade before its first challenge.

The fan-out is **one-hop and non-recursive**. It is triggered only by the original title takeover. Winning a second, third or later capability from that queued fan-out MUST NOT trigger another scan of the grade, another fan-out generation, or any recursive cascade. All secondary matches are siblings in the original takeover transaction and are individually adjudicated by their own capability batteries.

The fan-out MUST also respect the settled-matchup ledger. A sibling capability already settled between those same two checkpoint SHAs is skipped without inference and cannot be queued again.

Checkpoint-training origin does not partition title ownership. A whole-student checkpoint may independently earn titles in more than one grade, but only from complete comparable evidence for each target capability. The role is always attached to the capability's title grade, and a one-hop fan-out never leaves that title grade. School-wide strength alone creates no title and no fan-out entitlement.

A same-checkpoint examination may refresh current score or perfect-capability evidence without displacing itself, but it does not create a synthetic external title match. Simultaneous breadth remains the result of one comparable grade examination; it is never synthesized by unioning perfect scores from different cohorts.

This championship-only title-match requirement does not add a candidate-retention veto, change the teacher's policy, or relax graduation. Flash retains its existing deferred-title behavior.

## 4. Comparable evidence and decision order

Every promotion decision binds the intact student/AdamW checkpoint SHA and optimizer step; admitted graph and grade/capability set; versioned source and oracle; fixed cohort digest/seed; rubric and assessment scope digests; per-capability grade scores; incumbents' exact identities; and any incumbent-versus-contender same-cohort school outcomes. Cumulative numerator and denominator are mandatory **when an actual incumbent comparison occurs**; they are not invented for an uncontested, grade-only championship.

Different cohorts, school scopes, numeric test domains, source contracts, rubric versions or graph geometries are **different comparison strata**. Even matching denominators do not make two independent question sets directly comparable. A title challenge requires comparable evidence for the target capability; the one-hop fan-out requires comparable evidence for each additional same-grade capability it screens. An append-compatible historical graph may compete only where it can read the complete target panel and battery. If the required comparison cannot be made fairly, that displacement is unmeasured and MUST NOT be approved. Keep the older record as historical evidence.

The independent complete school exam owns graduation and may also supply capability-local championship evidence when its target panel is intact and comparable. Short paired parent/challenger sentinels own **provisional student update admission only**. Championship qualification cannot combine scores from different checkpoint identities or incomparable cohorts. Whole-student high-water scores are separately ratcheting recovery/diagnostic references, not automatic championship ownership. A successful local teaching update is not graduation.

Order: first compare the epoch's championship school identity with the live course/factory/semantic/model geometry and globally archive+clear all active roles/soups on any mismatch; then validate checkpoint provenance and custody; enumerate every title grade represented by complete measured capability evidence at that intact checkpoint; for each measured capability, check the settled-matchup ledger before any inference; if unseen, pair incumbent and contender on the target capability's current cohort and apply the superficial strict-gain-or-contender-ceiling qualification rule; only qualified first-time pairs enter a title match; freshly infer **both** incumbent and contender on the current cohort for latest championship evidence and on that capability's surviving battery for title adjudication; the incumbent retains the capability only by a strictly higher battery score, while a challenger win or tie transfers the title; refresh the winner's current score and surviving battery; preserve both the capability title grade and the winner checkpoint's retained-update origin grade; write the immutable settled-matchup receipt. If and only if the contender actually takes that original title, run one title-grade-local fan-out screen against remaining valid incumbents in that capability's grade, skipping already-settled pairs, queue matches where the new champion matches or beats their superficial scores, and adjudicate those sibling matches without recursion. Then commit immutable generation/outbox evidence and rebuild any affected soup. Never use cached neural predictions in title adjudication, never let an old peak score stand in for a live defense, never rematch a settled pair, never recurse fan-out, and never let the challenger become its own pre-promotion reference.

## 5. Frozen soups and teaching rights

When an **earned** role changes owner or covered capability count reaches a new maximum, rebuild the affected grade's frozen weights-only soup from **active earned** championship owners. Each grade capability contributes **one vote**. A checkpoint with multiple roles receives that many ingredient shares; it is loaded once but counted by role multiplicity.

A soup's base vote count is frozen with its recipe. Same-grade advice uses the full count. Soups remain the **only championship-derived cross-grade influence mechanism** and may retain the existing foreign-grade divisor.

Each active **maxed capability champion** contributes direct advisory influence only inside the capability's title grade. The vote belongs to the capability role, not the checkpoint file or the grade of the optimizer visit that created it. If one checkpoint owns several maxed roles in that title grade, load it once and count one vote per qualifying role. A non-max capability champion contributes zero direct votes, but it remains a valid soup ingredient and soup vote under the existing best-role contract. Individual champion roles cast **no direct votes outside their title grade**.

Direct and soup advice are restricted to independently source-gold-agreeing training examples, admitted answer types and the teacher's selected distillation strength with the bounded influence policy. Wrong mentor predictions contribute no advice for that example. Advice may not alter gold, exam answers, graduation, capability scheduling, the causal student's parameters or AdamW identity, or the teacher's selected lesson strategy. Never average AdamW moments, blend incompatible embedding geometries, or impose an assistant-selected prior-grade lesson proportion.

After an epoch reset, **old soups remain archived but inactive**. The baseline casts **zero votes and no direct advice**. Later newly earned roles can form new-epoch soups. A tie or refused takeover neither replaces an old incumbent in the active epoch nor silently triggers soup reconstruction.

## 6. Custody and human trial boundaries

Use existing immutable states, generation-bound outbox, protected bundle references, and post-commit retirement controls. Preserve the causal student, matching AdamW, teacher state, semantic version, source receipts, historic champions and soups. A temporary APFS experiment fork is review-pending and may only be retained or discarded after custody/provenance reconciliation. No implicit promotion of a fork into the canonical run.

Agents MAY run source checks, read-only current-run preflights, isolated disposable model and MLX fixture checks, and verified fork preparation. They MUST NOT start actual student optimizer updates or call the human-only neural training command. Only the human starts or resumes those updates.

## 7. Mandatory conformance tests

1. Reject false graduation assembled from mixed checkpoints or 99%-complete school scores. Successful graduation plus new admission resets **all active championships and grade soups to empty**, while retaining a single graduation-linked baseline as a **non-teaching** reference, without inventing mastery or deleting history.
2. Routine exams, candidate rejection and poor results **do not** reset the championship epoch.
3. An empty capability role accepts the first complete capability measurement at or above 66% and rejects a lower score. Its initial battery is exactly the questions that checkpoint answered correctly on that threshold-crossing measurement. Against an occupied role, a strict comparable capability-score gain qualifies the contender; reaching the capability ceiling also qualifies it even when the incumbent ties that ceiling. A below-ceiling superficial tie does not qualify it. Other roles owned by the same checkpoint cannot block admission to this one capability's title match.
4. Checkpoint-training origin is preserved as provenance but does not bar capability-title eligibility. A checkpoint last retained during A can win a B capability title from complete comparable B evidence. The active role remains a B title, B soup vote and B direct-advice role; any fan-out stays within B. No full-grade or whole-school takeover is required merely to challenge one capability.
5. Cohort, target-capability panel, rubric or incompatible graph mismatch rejects promotion. Append-compatible historical checkpoints may compete only on complete readable target panels and surviving batteries. New or stale cohort scores never get compared as raw numbers.
6. A legitimate battery-match takeover updates exact individual roles and rebuilds the affected soup with correct vote multiplicity. A contender that fails the superficial gate or loses/ties the battery match preserves role ownership; a completed defense may still refresh the incumbent's current role score and surviving battery.
7. Historic champions and soups become inactive at curriculum admission; **the baseline supplies zero adviser models and zero votes**. Newly earned maxed capability roles advise directly only within their title grade. Newly earned soups advise with full same-grade base votes and the foreign-grade divisor and remain the bounded cross-grade championship-derived influence mechanism. Non-max roles remain soup-only advisers. A non-training reset also clears then-active experimental titles and soups and leaves old artifacts archived.
8. The complete student, matching AdamW, teacher history and optimizer step remain unchanged by role reset, promotion or soup creation. Interrupted outbox delivery replays the same event without duplicate promotion.
9. The existing isolated fork passes read-only resume and source-bound inference before the human receives a test command. A locked atomic mid-grade empty-roster reset preserves native AdamW and all prior generations. No extra 8 GB copy is required. Fixture checks cannot establish real neural retention.
10. A capability title may be minted or challenged from complete capability-local evidence at one intact checkpoint; complete peer-capability scoring is not a prerequisite merely because the incumbent checkpoint owns several belts. Provenance, graph, rubric and source comparability still fail closed.
11. After one real same-grade title takeover, the winning checkpoint receives exactly one quick paired screen against the other incumbents in that grade. Every capability where it matches or beats the incumbent superficial score queues one sibling defended-title match. A win in any sibling match MUST NOT trigger another screen or queue generation.
12. A title action never fan-outs outside the target capability's grade. A checkpoint may own independently earned titles in multiple grades, but each title's direct champion advice remains local to that title grade. Cross-grade influence beyond those independently earned roles continues through frozen soups.

13. A superficially qualified contender and incumbent both perform fresh inference on the identical current cohort and identical incumbent surviving title battery. Neural predictions are never read from a cache for this adjudication. The incumbent retains the capability only with a strictly higher battery score. A challenger battery score that is equal to or greater than the incumbent's transfers the title. The winner's latest current-cohort replay supplies the role's displayed/current score even when it is lower than that checkpoint's historical peak. The next surviving battery is the deduplicated union of questions the winner answered correctly in the superficial gate, fresh current-cohort replay and prior battery replay. Missed historical questions remain archived but are not active obligations. Missing, altered or unverifiable question evidence fails closed.
14. The first completed title fight writes an immutable settled-matchup key for `(capability, unordered checkpoint pair)`. Any later attempt to pair those two checkpoint SHAs for that capability is skipped before championship inference and cannot change role score, owner or battery, even if either checkpoint's later exam score changes or ownership reverses.
15. Quarantine of B.AND, B.IFF or any other capability does not suppress a complete, source-valid title challenge for any other measured capability. Championship processing runs over every completely measured eligible capability regardless of scheduler grade or checkpoint-training origin; withheld capabilities simply do not mint/challenge from absent scores.
16. Changing any component of the championship school identity archives and clears **all** active roles and **all** active soups before they can advise or defend under the new geometry. The causal student/own optimizer continue; championship history stays immutable; live championship authority restarts empty.

## 8. Runtime implementation and test-readiness rule

The historical `capability_frontier.graduation_perfect` retains its original breadth-only record format. The **active post-reset championship** is governed by `epoch_championships`: every epoch is bound to one exact championship school identity; any identity drift archives and clears all active roles/soups before further use. Ordinary roles are current defended-title roles with capability-title jurisdiction separated from checkpoint-training provenance, capability-local superficial admission, fresh defended batteries, permanent pairwise matchup idempotence and one-hop title-grade-local fan-out after a real takeover. The role receipt must preserve both the title grade and `checkpoint_origin_grade`, plus immutable historical provenance, mutable-by-succession surviving title battery and latest defended current-cohort score; the epoch retains immutable settled-matchup receipts. In `trainer._take_exam`, every measured title grade in a normal fresh exam is eligible for capability-local championship processing regardless of scheduler grade or optimizer-origin grade. Only independently earned roles and their newly frozen title-grade soups enter active advice. Each active maxed role contributes direct capability influence only in its title grade. A frozen soup gives one vote per active capability role, including a best-below-max role, and remains eligible for the existing foreign-grade divisor only while the school identity is unchanged. Shared direct-role checkpoints may share one loaded immutable weight object for efficiency, but every first-ever title-match inference is fresh and must not reuse cached predicted answers. Deterministic source/oracle results may remain memoized independently. All advice remains independently source-gold-gated. Old `graduation_perfect` and `grade_soups` stay archived and protected from retirement. A-to-B and B-to-C admission initialize a **non-teaching** graduation baseline with no titles. An explicit administrative reset or holistic school-identity change archives any current-epoch roles/soups and starts empty. The teacher and single causal student still own their original policy, parameters and AdamW.

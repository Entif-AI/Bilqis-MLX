# A/B classroom qualification and practical five-warning instrumentation

**Date:** 2026-10-16. **Authority:** ADR-031, protected `IPR-0220` issue [#336](https://github.com/socr8s/private-ip/issues/336), still **OPEN**. **Baseline:** `codex/336-ab-diagnostic-qualification` at `c8ec59e670584068e0de71e189ed8e68fcb0e894`. **Implementation worktree:** `codex/336-ab-classroom-continuation`. This document describes an engineering verification unit, not an actual-student graduation.

## The classroom contract

When the student starts a grade, draw a single independently source-scored exam. Its *exact questions, scoring rubric and graduation criteria* remain fixed through all failures and recovery rounds. Once the student passes and later returns to that grade in the A→B→... spiral, generate a **new** exam. Reuse incorrect exam questions with their gold answers for supervised teaching when helpful. **No sealed exam-question exclusion exists.** Gold never enters the model's scoring inference. Full same-checkpoint 100% graduation, native AdamW custody and human-only actual neural training remain unchanged.

Do not demand that an early-grade learner infer unfamiliar compositions from several grades ahead. Detect failures to teach and learn the prescribed source-valid content. Specimen-level research provenance can improve causal attribution later; it is not required to activate these practical warnings.

## Executable instrumentation and its limits

| Warning | Trigger/evidence | Current behavior and scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S1. Repetitive model answers | Reconstructed or in-memory scored predictions against source gold with both classes | Source-verified capability, grade and whole-student observations; flags constant answers or repeated such behavior across exams |
| S2. Chance/lagging performance | Correctness against actual per-capability majority-class baselines, with longitudinal consecutive warnings | Emits warnings at each exam boundary and in the read-only saved-run audit. Relative peer/champion lag and statistical uncertainty calibration remain open |
| S3. Gains do not last | The **same** fixed qualifying exam scored on at least three distinct whole-student checkpoints | Captures initial, intermediate, latest score and flags absent gain or later regression from a peak. A newly sampled exam begins a new series; score persistence is not a causal explanation of instruction |
| S4. New semantics disconnected from earlier ones | Approved course prerequisite IDs and the source operation subtrees in **selected and executed** optimizer batches | Flags visits where no admitted predecessor appears. Direct A capabilities with no declared earlier prerequisites are `NOT_APPLICABLE`. Does not demand original ID of every single instructional specimen |
| S5. Needlessly complicated introductions | First optimizer batch of a full-grade introduction compared with simplest available source-oracled **offered** examples (semantic operation count, then token length) | Flags when the teacher selected only more complex available examples. This measures missed simpler options, not the global mathematical minimum. Recovery-only visits are `NOT_APPLICABLE` |

The original adaptive teacher still selects its legacy UCB strategy. The instrument neither forces an earlier-grade lesson ratio nor blocks source-valid training when a future structural dialect cannot be inspected: missing diagnostic evidence becomes `NOT_MEASURED`. Each selected visit now receives a typed `bilqis.teacher-feedback/v1` receipt with the exact inherited policy key, active capability/grade warning context, source-valid opportunities, the delivered action counts, a stable decision digest, and the methodology evidence actually seen. The receipt explicitly records `policy_selection.origin=INHERITED_LEGACY_UCB` and `diagnostic_context_consumed=false`; these warnings are causal context and operator evidence, not a false claim that the old UCB already learned a diagnostic-conditioned option policy.

Previously failed source-valid exam constructions persist across newly sampled cohorts until a current student recheck shows them corrected. Replay capacity is derived from the unresolved backlog, remaining visit budget, and answer classes available in the already selected batch; there is no fixed 25-percent or quarter-batch rule. Exposure counts make starvation inspectable, and a post-visit prediction check immediately removes corrected mistakes while retaining still-wrong ones. Current-grade champion mistakes remain separately receipted teacher-vote candidates and do not masquerade as the historical `old` policy dimension.

At an exam boundary, the same receipt chain records a teacher-outcome window. A single intervening action for a capability on the same fixed qualification cohort gets an exact before/after score delta. Multiple intervening actions, missing scores, a first exam with no fixed baseline, or a fresh exam after grade re-enrollment are explicitly non-attributable. The compact per-visit and exam summaries travel in the **existing committed generation event outbox**, not a parallel item-ID ledger. All five signals remain separate from proof that the model has truly learned or retained source semantics.

## Remaining work under #336

The originally requested *fully generic* code-owned semantic/lesson catalog, dynamic-law admission and complete cross-world coverage remain unfinished; earlier audit identified 33 unresolved typed-but-not-source-proven composition positions. Existing A/B prescribed source-verified lessons need not be blocked for those future expansions. Peer/champion-relative and statistically calibrated S2 warnings remain open.

One policy admission remains deliberately unresolved. Historical source review confirmed that teacher `old` means a count of **separate earlier-grade-origin questions** in a 32-row batch, `preview` means separate future-grade rows, and `distill` is a KL coefficient against a frozen pre-attempt parent on retention rows. Mapping any of those inherited knobs to champion-voted current questions, exam-mistake replay, or embedded prerequisite operands would change their meaning and corrupt historical UCB credit. A future versioned teacher-option policy may consume S1–S5 and learn distinct option credit only after explicit policy/reward admission under #336. This branch preserves the learned teacher state and records the evidence needed to compare such a policy without silently introducing it.

A human-operated A/B experiment can now exercise the fixed-exam warning and receipt machinery, but empirical learning remains unproven. Human training must demonstrate acquisition, A→B retention, and 144/144 cumulative completion before any Grade C admission. No new Grade C semantics or actual student optimization was attempted in this engineering slice.

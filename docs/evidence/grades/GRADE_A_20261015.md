# Grade A: from fixed-example plateau to generated semantic graduation

**Evidence observed:** 2026-10-15 local time. **Release:**
`bilqis.mlx.grade-a-live/20261015`. **Status:** graduated under the admitted
Grade A rule, awaiting a separately authorized next grade. This is a
retrospective of *human-operated* MLX training, not an agent training receipt or
a claim of unrestricted New Ithkuil competence.

## The result, with the actual comparison

The run's final committed school receipt reports `GRADE_A_MASTERED_AWAITING_GRADE_B`
and stage `AWAITING_NEXT_GRADE` at optimizer step **153,144**. Its complete
student, Grade A champion and six capability-best roles point to the **same**
checkpoint bundle, `bundles/best-2274-7f1b14e6ed72`, whose full native
FP32-model-plus-AdamW safetensors SHA-256 is
`d25b8715cf30bb9166781916eba019e0aeefe339a8ec020e751a9958d980aaad`.
The generator supplied a recorded, source-checked cohort of 72 questions,
12 per capability. The *one checkpoint* answered all 72 correctly:

| Admitted Grade A capability | Final sampled assessment |
| --- | ---: |
| `A.IDENTITY` | 12/12 |
| `A.PARENT` | 12/12 |
| `A.LINK` | 12/12 |
| `A.MEMBER` | 12/12 |
| `A.EDGE` | 12/12 |
| `A.MAPS` | 12/12 |
| **Complete current Grade A rubric** | **72/72** |

The decisive paired observation is not the dashboard's first-versus-latest
number. On the **identical final cohort** (SHA-256
`671c6c52088beb5603c6dfca1630afe737985b02eafc7d9d797601bf00e39742`),
the preceding retained incumbent at optimizer step **153,000** scored **71/72**:
all capabilities were perfect except `A.MAPS` at 11/12. The candidate at
step **153,144** scored **72/72**, including `A.MAPS` at 12/12. That is a
one-item, same-cohort improvement between two independently loaded whole
checkpoints. The result also satisfies the existing admission rule: 100% of
the comprehensive grade sample at one complete student-and-optimizer state.

The six capabilities are typed Boolean operations grounded in the original
pasigraphy graph. Grade A does **not** certify direct numeric-answer mastery,
New Ithkuil surface-form legality, arbitrary semantic composition, the next
grade, cross-grade retention or whole-school completion. No independent
sentinel, calibration/error margin or unrestricted transfer metric was
measured in this result.

## Measurement boundaries and elapsed time

The latest human-run console ends with `GRADE_A_AWAITING_NEXT_GRADE` and records
**402 completed visits in 198.7 seconds**, ending at step 153,144. The
operator independently described the training run as taking *less than two
minutes*. The preserved terminal stop receipt measures approximately **3
minutes 18.7 seconds** for its recorded invocation. These are distinct source
claims, not numbers to average or silently harmonize; the shorter observation
may use a different start/stop boundary and lacks an attached clock trace.

The latest saved student has **2,274 total visits**, **6,562 adaptive-teacher
observations** and generation **2,845**. Relative to the explicitly pinned
original model at optimizer step 116,760 and inherited teacher at 4,288 events,
the full school lineage accumulated **36,384 optimizer steps** (2,274 visits
times 16 steps) and **2,274 additional teacher observations**. The latest
402-visit invocation accounts for **6,432 optimizer steps**, beginning from
step 146,712. Its first generated complete sample was **38/72 at step
146,808**; the latest generated sample was **72/72 at step 153,144**.

Those two samples were generated with different seeds and question identities.
Their 34-item raw score difference describes the observed endpoints, not a
matched-question 34-item gain. Each sample is a valid same-checkpoint
criterion when complete, but a distributional estimate needs repeated
independent, source-checked cohorts and explicit variance reporting. The
final **71/72 versus 72/72** comparison above *is* paired: both checkpoints
were evaluated on the same saved final cohort.

The old phase's last examination was **37/72 at step 146,712**, the handoff

These facts strongly identify an operationally consequential change in the
learning process, but they do not isolate which constituent of that change
was sufficient.

The source-instruction figures are especially revealing: the old teacher
typically assessed a pool it already answered almost perfectly *before*
each visit, leaving a narrow and often flat reward signal. Fresh source
construction repeatedly gave the learner more errors to repair and the
adaptive teacher more measurable improvement to observe. Do not interpret
the lower new-phase in-sample accuracy as a worse learner: it measures a
newly sampled, materially less saturated instruction distribution.

The final native bundle readback verified **128,936,784 bytes** of safetensors,

arrays (119 tensors), with optimizer step **153,144** and a matching 222-token
semantic catalog. The run retains one final whole-student bundle, pointed to
by the current student, its Grade A champion and all six capability-best roles.

## What we inherited and what failed first

The model did not begin from random weights. Bilqis inherited a complete
222-token original PyTorch student, its FP32 parameters and matching native
AdamW moments at step 116,760, together with an adaptive teacher whose
4,288 historical observations originated at step 116,952. The mismatch in
teacher-source and model-source steps is explicit provenance, not evidence
of a synthetic same-step checkpoint. The native semantic graph remains
version-pinned; no approved token identity was silently replaced.

The experimental Grade A release originally assigned 24 prebuilt training
questions and 12 fixed, permanently disjoint examination questions to each of
six predicates: **144 training and 72 assessment examples**. The trainer
sampled its instructional questions repeatedly from those same 24 examples,
often reaching near-perfect training accuracy and little or no remaining
adaptive reward. Yet the early human-operated examinations did not advance
materially. The first source-bound read-only diagnosis records 12 fixed 72-item
exams after 48 visits, ranging between **31 and 38 correct**, while instruction
accuracy frequently reached 100%. The first pinned baseline was **34/72**.

That combination is the central failure evidence. The *teaching procedure*
was fitting a narrow finite example pool while qualification measured a
different fixed pool. Repeated optimizer updates on the finite training
inventory were not repairing the failing constructions at sufficient scale.
The original rationale for reserving examinations, including the presumed
methodological rigor of never teaching those identities, had become a
pedagogical constraint rather than a demonstrated benefit to this student.

This is not a retroactive assertion that the former fixed assessment bytes
were corrupted. The legacy manifests and grades remain valid records of what
that earlier system measured. The mistake lay in elevating question secrecy
and static partition ownership above the user's goal: robust semantic
acquisition through richly connected, repeatedly reconstructed lessons.

## The user's course corrections and their engineering consequences

**1. Teach concepts, not a protected stockpile of question strings.** The
user rejected permanent sealed holdouts as a prerequisite for training and
generalization. The same source-defined predicate constructors now produce
both instructional examples and scheduled assessments. Every training visit,
and every optimizer batch within a visit, generates seeded variations in
world identity, relation structure, truth class and admitted world size. The
teacher may revisit an examination construction *after scoring*, including
the precise examples the student got wrong. No answer is disclosed to the
student during the examination itself.

**2. Preserve formal semantics and source oracles.** Random wording is not
the educational unit. The admitted constructor uses the historical typed
pasigraphy record format and model token IDs. Each generated question must
agree with the predecessor's two evaluators and the independent Grade A
oracle, remain within the model's context/vocabulary and satisfy its intended
TRUE/FALSE truth class. Teaching and testing share the generator, so the
curriculum covers the same semantic algebra rather than a disjoint set of
questions authored by another mechanism. The six Grade A operations have not
been redefined.

**3. Use mistakes to choose subsequent instruction.** The generated exam is a
read-only evaluation interval, not a place to optimize on exposed gold
answers. Once it is scored, failures become teacher-priority lesson material.
The implementation preserves distinct failed specimens, balances replacement
examples by their answer class, and rotates a bounded miss window across
steps/visits so repeated mistakes do not disappear behind a permanently
preferred first subset. The inherited teacher still chooses its existing
strategies and receives observed before/after source-training rewards.
It was not replaced with a new teaching algorithm.

**4. Compare checkpoint candidates fairly rather than demanding a fixed
exam forever.** Each assessment materializes an immutable seeded *cohort*,
including item content, generator identity, original source, expected golds
and a content digest. The currently learning student and every distinct
retained incumbent model are then evaluated on those *same* questions before
capability-best promotion. A changing cohort must never be mistaken for
an identical rubric's matched-question improvement. The semantic contract
and item-count requirement are stable; the cohort SHA identifies the actual
sample and its replay. This distinction prevents score drift from selecting
champions based on incomparable random questions.

**5. Save entire students and optimizer state, not model soups or isolated
capability fragments.** A single complete FP32 checkpoint may own several
capability-best roles. The graduation vote requires all six capabilities
at the same checkpoint, not six different snapshots cherry-picked per skill.
Superseded unreferenced candidates may be pruned only after durable pointer
publication and verified custody; current student, champion and still-needed
historical pointers remain protected. Saved human-run migration retains the
whole original student, its AdamW step, the adaptive teacher observations
and legacy receipts. It does not zero out successful training because a
curriculum version changes.

**6. Keep the operator's control and original recovery discipline.** Only a
human-supplied `--human-run` invokes the actual optimizer. The existing
six-panel School50 display, grade scheduler, 16-step visit cadence, teacher
policies and 100 GiB disk reserve remain in force. The repair also made
state-pointer recovery idempotent, staged complete bundle writes outside
the live champion directory, and quarantined possibly recoverable model
files left between publication and pointer updates. These repairs protect
the state rather than enlarging the curriculum.

## What the evidence supports and what remains uncertain

The original static-pool procedure repeatedly reached good local teaching
scores while failing to qualify the complete grade. Under the replacement
source-generated procedure, a subsequently resumed human-owned student
reached the admitted **72/72** condition and obtained the Grade A champion.
This result strongly supports the user's diagnosis that the static-example
policy was a practical obstacle to acquisition and correction. It shows an
operationally successful course correction, not merely a prettier dashboard.

It does **not** isolate a single numerical treatment effect. The final model
had already undergone many earlier optimizer updates, adaptive-teacher
observations and checkpoint selections; the new lesson distribution, explicit
error replay and changing cohort measurement arrived together. No
equal-compute randomized A/B experiment was run, nor does one perfect 72-item
sample estimate failure probability across all legal semantic worlds.
The leading explanation is the transition from a memorized finite training
inventory to repeated verified semantic construction with actionable
post-exam feedback. A proper subsequent research evaluation can measure
coverage by semantic factor, retention across fresh cohorts, robustness to
renaming/reordering, response-label shortcuts and interference when the next
grade is added. None of those experiments justifies reinstating a permanent
teaching embargo.

Most importantly, neither the current binary grade certificate nor high
source-training rewards certify that the student can emit a direct numeral or
a grammatical New Ithkuil wordform. The independently tested typed numeric,
five-state evidence and full-vocabulary semantic-token adapters remain
separate research tools, not hidden extra Grade A achievements.

## The constraint for Grade B and later grades

Grade B must extend **the admitted semantic curriculum**, not substitute a
new learning architecture. Reuse the current generated teaching, complete
seeded cohorts, post-exam feedback, same-cohort whole-checkpoint selection,
teacher, six-panel telemetry and durable model/AdamW custody. The existing
`school.append_next_grade()` enforces exactly one new grade and preserves the
published Grade A `Grade` object unchanged.

The canonical Curriculum Registry currently calls its older B01–B07 lessons
*historically trained, mastery unverified*. Its `Grade Tranches` B row is
*continuity / unchanged by user*, with no newly approved constructions. Its
structured prerequisite table has no released Grade B lesson membership.
An earlier plan independently names **C** as its next *proposed* training
tranche after the preserved historical A/B lineage. These identities must
not be conflated: the newly graduated six-predicate MLX Grade A is neither
the historical G→A aggregate nor evidence that historical B is already
mastered. Grade B content must be formally rebound to its source-backed
operations and independently verified prerequisite/answer castes before
being admitted into the live run.

The minimal safe implementation path is to publish a source-verified B
`Grade` and its constructor/oracle coverage, append that exact one grade
at `AWAITING_NEXT_GRADE`, and route only the newly admitted capability
semantics through the existing human trainer. This work must not alter
Grade A's definitions, reset its champion, rewrite its school history,
introduce a new teacher loop, or activate unverified New Ithkuil roots.
Until that admission exists, the current saved student must stay at
`AWAITING_NEXT_GRADE`. A description in a spreadsheet or a historical B01
label is not an executable neural-training authorization.

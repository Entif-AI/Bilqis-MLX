## Implemented source-fact subset

`compile_source_constructive_lessons` derives source-identity
teaching and exact attested-relation recognition from the frozen
A01 census. Each emitted immutable lesson records a positive
source fact, a different source-valid contrast, canonical answer
modality, independently executable checker reference, source
constituents, prerequisite source closure, variation axes, release
and source-evidence digest. The checker reconstructs the source
fact from the authoritative frozen Atlas, not from generator
gold or English glosses. Forged fact/foreign-release replay fails.

`compile_semantic_curriculum` carries these source-only lessons
alongside its complete frozen denominator. Source lesson emission
does not mark any source-family obligation `ADAPTER_READY`,
`EXPOSED` or `QUALIFIED`; all learner outcomes remain
`LEARNING_UNVERIFIED`.

Real pinned corpus: **9,307 source-identity lessons** generated
from **9,307 source-eligible ABI identities**. The remaining
**3,044** family obligations have no source-only lesson program
yet. The independent relation checker is demonstrated on a
synthetic fixture with two separately attested source relations;
the pinned corpus currently has no required concept-backed
relation-recognition family obligation.

## Explicit unfinished acceptance

This is **not** a complete A08.1b turn-in. Source-attested
Boolean/numeric laws, morphology composition/decomposition,
scope/modality/time and released transformations still need
their source-owned constructors and independent typed checkers,
plus binding into the existing `ConstructionProgram`,
`CapabilityDefinition` and Factory registry. No semantic law
was invented from an English gloss or a table name. These
missing implementations remain mandatory delivery debt; A08.2,
A08.3 and parent coverage cannot claim completion from this
partial software artifact.

## Material semantic corrections

1. **Boolean and task semantics.** Historical `bilqis_logic/kernel.py` defines typed constants and truth tables; `task.equivalent` checks all P/Q assignments. The NI lexicon `-ŇŢT-` BSC stem 2 is a more precise *evaluation-related* root than generic abstract operation for `task.evaluate`. NI `-Č-` stem 2 supplies a functional-equivalence family, but does not prove a universally quantified truth table. NI negation/coordination/conditional morphology may help express concepts; they are not automatically typed `NOT/AND/OR/XOR/XNOR/IMPLIES/IFF`.
2. **Formal quantifiers and named referents.** `micro.quantifier.EXISTS/FORALL` quantify over a fixed finite domain, and `micro.bound.0/1` use de Bruijn lexical depth, not ordinary pronoun identity. `A..E`, `S0..S2`, `J0..J2`, `K0/K1`, `F0/F1`, `E0..E3` are distinct typed referents. NI `-S-` BSC stem 2 is an official carrier for a named abstract entity; `-PS-` BSC stem 3 specifically denotes an event; `-NTHW-` OBJ denotes a mathematical function. NI `-ŇTÇ-` in its 'proposition' entry concerns a **proposal**, not formal truth-evaluable propositions.
3. **Generic graph kinship.** `PARENT/CHILD/GRANDPARENT` operate on finite generic entities and local graph rules. NI `-MP-` identifies functional kinship; `-MM-` genetic kinship. Neither is the generic graph predicate. `-RMC-` linking also does not automatically equal undirected abstract graph adjacency.
4. **Finite proofs are not unrestricted entailment.** `micro.proof.entails` checks its current finite-world closure; `micro.proof.rule_step` verifies a specific two-premise declared Horn rule; `micro.proof.countermodel` witnesses true premises and false conclusion in the particular current world. `development.op.FORMAL_ENTAILS` checks the truth of a declared proposition in that same closure. Neither entailment operation proves general `Gamma entails phi` across all models. NI `-MSL-` denotes inferential thought, not the entire formal proof certificate.
5. **Evidence-pair semantics.** `micro.evidence.NOT` swaps support/refutation, `AND` combines support conjunctively and refutation disjunctively, `OR` does the reverse; `UNKNOWN=(0,0)`, `CONFLICT=(1,1)`, while `AMBIGUOUS` means outcomes diverge *across active interpretations*. NI Validation reports the basis for an assertion, not this paraconsistent evidence calculus. `-MŢT-` demonstration cannot make a report automatically true.
6. **Mathematical sets, functions and paths.** `MEMBER/SUBSET/INTERSECTION/DIFFERENCE` have extensional set semantics; `DOMAIN/RANGE` are actual partial-function input and image sets; `INVERSE` rejects noninjective maps; `COMPOSITION` applies the second function after the first. `NEIGHBORS` means outgoing neighbors; `PATH` is a positive-length directed reachability relation, *not* a physical route. NI `-ŇZX-` supplies abstract structure/operation scaffolding, but not automatically a dedicated root for every operation.
7. **Event history and provenance.** `DERIVED_FROM` and `DEPENDENCY` use transitive closure over distinct edge kinds. `SUPERSEDES` is a direct same-proposition version edge even without changing report polarity; `REVISES` additionally requires a changed polarity. `IS_CURRENT` checks whether an event was superseded; `CURRENT_STATE` aggregates active reports and `HISTORICAL_STATE` all reports. NI `-ÇN-` concerns origination and `-XP-` replacement; neither subsumes these typed ledger predicates.
